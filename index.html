Weekly Updates

What's new in Exodus Dirt Systems, week by week. Newest first.

Week of Oct 18 – Oct 24, 2026

New Features

  1. Preferences now persist. Your selection for every preference is remembered from power cycle to power cycle.
  2. Database tables for all current entities. Every entity created in the app is now saved and persisted.
  3. Self-propelled tile plow is editable. Edit your self-propelled tile plow right in the app.
  4. Recording in the Pipe As-Installed design. As-installed designs can now be recorded as you work.
  5. Line POI. Record a line, then associate a point along it. It shows up as a road cone.
  6. Required Depth from Line POIs. Line POIs can be used in line recreation to force a Required Depth station along the line in the 3D profile.
  7. Pipe Inventory. Keep track of your pipe inventory in the app.
  8. Quick-Set remembers your last pipe. The next pipe you create uses the same pipe as last time. If you used 4", the next design starts with 4".
  9. Quick-Set by Pipe Role. New presets for Main, Lateral, and Sub-Lateral let you save constraints for all three independently. Each switches to its preset when that pipe is used.
  10. Long-press status items. Saved per run-screen operation (such as tile plow). Move them, rescale them, or delete them.

How it works

  1. Connect any compliant GPS receiver to your phone or tablet.
  2. Design your tile, leveling, or earthwork plan in the app.
  3. Build with confidence in the field.

Easy to use is our mantra. Less setup, more productive time on the ground.
