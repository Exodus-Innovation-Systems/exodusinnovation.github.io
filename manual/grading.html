Grading and as-installed

Grade with guidance you can read at a glance.

Activate a design and the screen turns into a grade guide: a bar down the side, a profile card at the bottom, and a countdown to the next change. Then record what you actually installed.

Colored grade bar

Green when you are on grade, orange and red as you move away.

Countdown like a parking sensor

Pulsing chevrons on the ground warn you before a reducer, required depth or the end of the run.

A record of what went in the ground

Grading to a design: activating a gradeable

A gradeable is anything the plow can grade to: a recorded 3D line, or a pipe design. Only one is active at a time.

Activate one

  1. Switch the top bar to Grading.
  2. Tap the metal handle on the left edge to open the left panel. Until something is active it reads No design is activated to grade to.
  3. Under Grade to, tap the design. Lines are grouped under 3D Lines, and pipe designs under their own group.

If the list is empty it says No gradeable designs in this project - record a 3D line or design a pipe.

What it looks like

The chosen design gets a check mark in the list, and the panel at the top changes to Bit grade (the plow's elevation error) with the Target elevation and Current elevation.

With the panel closed:

  • The design is drawn on the ground as a blue line with a yellow tolerance band (dashed black edges).
  • A grade bar down the left edge shows the error as colored blocks: green when you are on grade, orange and red as you move away (red is a large error).
  • A profile card at the bottom shows a side view of the design. It is titled with the design name and gives the Error, the distance From start, the Boot pitch and the Design slope. It also says how far you are off line when the machine is not on the design. The bit is drawn as a marker (BIT) with the error on a flag beside it.
  • The boot button switches from OFF to READY.

To stop grading to a design, tap the Deactivate button at the top right of the Grade to list. It shows only while a design is active. Tapping the active design again does the same. The design's lines are taken off the map. A design is also deactivated when you change project.

Grading options

In Grading mode the right-hand handle opens Grading Options.

ItemWhat it does
Large offsetHow far the double-arrow buttons raise or lower the grade each press.
Small offsetHow far the single-arrow buttons raise or lower the grade each press.
Current offsetThe total offset applied to the design grade. The middle button clears it to zero.

Countdown to a station while grading

When a pipe design is activated and the machine is moving along it, the screen warns about the next station ahead.

  • A pill at the top of the map names it and gives the distance, for example 152 MM → 102 MM - 33.28 m for a reducer. Other stations read Required depth with its depth, Pipe change, or End of run.
  • A group of three orange chevrons is drawn on the ground at the station.
  • The chevrons pulse slowly until you are within 30 m, then quicker, like a parking sensor.
  • For a reducer the chevrons narrow when the pipe gets smaller and widen when it gets bigger, so you can see which way it changes.

The station shown is the next one in the direction the machine is travelling.

In the screenshot the machine is 25.67 m from a reducer where the pipe changes from 152 mm to 102 mm. Two things tell the operator to be ready to switch pipe:

  • The pill at the top of the screen counts down the distance, here 152 MM → 102 MM - 25.67 m. It counts down as you drive toward it.
  • On the ground at the station, the three chevrons narrow toward the bottom (the pipe is getting smaller) and a label reads 152 mm → 102 mm. Because the machine is inside 30 m, the chevrons are pulsing faster.

When you reach the station, change to the new pipe.

Recording the pipe as installed

Record installed pipe logs the path of the plow bit while you lay pipe against a design, so the project holds a record of what was actually put in the ground, as well as what was designed. It is for a pipe design (not a plain 3D line).

Where it is

Under the boot button (bottom right) is a thin bar, the record chip. It is the button for pipe recording. It sits under the boot, because the bit is what is being recorded.

ChipMeaning
Faint, very thinNothing to record against. No pipe design is activated, or the boot has no position yet.
Grey barReady. A pipe design is activated.
Red, with a pulsing dot and RECRecording.
Red and blinking, NO FIXRecording, but no position has come in for the last few seconds. Recorded points are only taken when the fix is good enough (see the required quality in your survey settings).
Amber, LINKWaiting for grade control to engage (see Start automatically when grade control engages).

What you need first

  1. Switch to Grading.
  2. Activate a pipe design under Grade to (see Grading to a design: activating a gradeable). Pipe designs are listed under their own Pipe designs group above the 3D lines.
  3. Choose the pipe you are laying (below).

Choose the pipe you are laying

The recording is tagged with the pipe you say you are laying, so you must say which one.

  1. Touch and hold the record chip. The Record Installed Pipe dialog opens.
  2. Recording to shows the activated pipe design.
  3. Under Pipe being laid, tap the box and choose the pipe from your pipe inventory. Pipes of the same role as the design (main, lateral or sub-lateral) are listed first. If the box is greyed out, your inventory is empty: add pipes first (see Pipe inventory).
  4. Tap Close.

You can change the pipe in the middle of a run (for example when you switch to a smaller pipe at a reducer): open the dialog again and pick the new pipe. What was recorded so far is kept with the old pipe, and the rest with the new one.

Start and stop

  • Tap the record chip to start recording by hand. The chip turns red and shows REC.
  • Tap it again to stop.
  • While recording, the dialog also shows Recorded this pass, the length so far.

The record is saved to the project as you go (roughly every second), so a crash loses very little. Leaving the run screen ends the recording and saves it.

Start automatically when grade control engages

In the dialog, turn on Start recording when grade control engages. The chip then turns amber and shows LINK.

  • When you engage grade control (Auto), recording starts by itself.
  • When you disengage, recording stops.
  • Each new engagement starts a new recording.
  • While linked, a tap on the chip while engaged switches that recording off until the next engagement, and a second tap puts it back on.
  • The chip still starts by hand while grade control is not engaged.

The "switch pipe" prompt

If the design has a pipe change (a reducer) ahead, a banner appears above the chip while you are recording, for example Design: switch to 102 mm HDPE corrugated 4in in 12 m. When you reach it, change the pipe and tap Switched. This tells the record that you are now laying the pipe the design calls for. (It is the same as choosing the pipe in the dialog.)

Tip

The countdown pill and chevrons described in Countdown to a station while grading tell you where the change is. The banner and Switched button are how you say you have done it.
