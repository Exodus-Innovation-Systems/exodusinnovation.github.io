A one-page lookup: say what you want to do, and see where to find it.

Quick reference: where to find every feature

I want to...Go to...
Record the master benchmarkThe banner at the top of the run screen.
Record another benchmarkRight panel, Set Benchmark.
Mark a spotRight panel, Record Marker (tap = snapshot, hold = averaged).
Record a line while drivingRight panel, Line Recording.
Mark a spot on a line (road cone)Right panel, Line Recording, required-depth button while recording (see Points of interest on a line).
Collect points one at a timeRight panel, Point to Point, or the rod and Capture.
Record terrainRight panel, Terrain Recording switch.
See the terrain as a color mapRight-hand column, layers button. Hold to change opacity.
Change contour spacingRight-hand column, hold the eye button.
Watch speed, heading, elevation or satellites on the mapRight-hand column, speedometer button (see Status panels).
Record the ground under the boot receiver instead of the receiver heightBottom right, hold the small tab above the boot button (see Survey height offset).
Log what pipe was actually laidUnder the boot button: tap the chip to record, hold it to choose the pipe (see Recording the pipe as installed).
Check GPS fix qualityTop bar, tap the GPS icon.
Change the GPS sourceTop bar, hold the GPS icon.
Connect correctionsTop bar, corrections icon.
Hide or show layersLeft panel check boxes.
Rebench to a benchmarkLeft panel, expand Benchmarks, tap the rebench arrow (see Rebench).
Diagnose a poor fixTop bar, hold the GPS icon to open GPS Diagnostics (see GPS Diagnostics).
Shoot points on footRod button, then Capture.
